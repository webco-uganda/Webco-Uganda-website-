About WEBCO

Who We Are

Western Empowerment Believers Community Organisation and its community-focused vision.

Our Purpose

Empowering communities with faith and purpose.

WEBCO is structured around faith education, social development and sustainable initiatives that improve livelihoods and promote positive transformation.

Phase I Area of Focus

Ntungamo, Kabarole, Ibanda and Kanungu.

Mission

To empower communities through faith education, social development and sustainable initiatives that improves livelihoods and promote positive transformation.

Motto
