Welcome to WEBCO

Faith-driven action for stronger communities.

WEBCO exists to empower communities through faith education, social development and sustainable initiatives that improve livelihoods and promote positive transformation.

Our work is designed to bring people together, strengthen opportunity and support practical community-led development across Western Uganda.
